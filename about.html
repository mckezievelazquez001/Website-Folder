Artist Statement

Mckenzie Velazquez is a photographer and multidisciplinary artist based in Miami, Florida. Her practice is rooted in the act of observation and a curiosity for the world around her. Through film and digital photography she explores the relationship between people and their environments with an attention to color and form. Her work often returns to themes of history and spirituality as well as the quiet intimacies of everyday life. She approaches photography as a way of understanding the present and preserving what might otherwise be forgotten.
